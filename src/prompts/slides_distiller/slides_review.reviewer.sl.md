### 2. Pedagogical Balance
Evaluate the teaching effectiveness:

**Content Pacing**:
- Are complex topics given adequate slides to develop ideas gradually?
- Are simple topics appropriately concise?
- Is there good flow between topics?

**Teaching Approach Alignment**:
- Do mathematical topics have proper equation presentation (not too cramped)?
- Do visual topics have appropriate diagram support?
- Do practical topics include sufficient examples?

**Cognitive Load**:
- Are high-cognitive-load concepts broken into digestible chunks?
- Is there appropriate scaffolding (building from simple to complex)?
- Are there good transition/summary slides between major sections?

**Output**: ✓ GOOD / ⚠ NEEDS IMPROVEMENT / ✗ POOR
**Strengths**: [What works well]
**Weaknesses**: [What needs improvement]
**Specific recommendations**: [Which slides to adjust and how]

---

### 3. Visual Coverage
Assess the use of visual elements:

**Visual Assignment Quality**:
- Does every slide that needs a visual have one assigned?
- Are visual assignments appropriate for the content?
- Are visuals from inventory used effectively?

**Visual Variety**:
- Is there good mix of diagrams, equations, examples, etc.?
- Are any visual types overused or underused?
- Do consecutive slides avoid visual repetition?

**Gap Handling**:
- Are missing visuals clearly specified?
- Are suggestions for creating new visuals clear and actionable?
- Are alternative resources proposed when needed?

**Visual-Text Balance**:
- Are heavily visual slides not too sparse on explanation?
- Are text-heavy slides broken up appropriately?
- Is there good integration of visuals with content?

**Output**: ✓ EXCELLENT / ✓ GOOD / ⚠ NEEDS WORK / ✗ INSUFFICIENT
**Strengths**: [Effective visual usage]
**Issues**: [Visual gaps or problems]
**Recommendations**: [Specific visual improvements needed]

---

### 4. Content Quality
Review the actual slide content:

**Text Clarity**:
- Is text concise but complete (not just bullet points where explanation is needed)?
- Are definitions clear and precise?
- Are key concepts properly emphasized?

**Mathematical Content**:
- Are equations properly formatted and explained?
- Is mathematical notation consistent?
- Are derivations shown at appropriate level of detail?

**Examples and Applications**:
- Are examples well-chosen and illustrative?
- Do practical slides show concrete implementations?
- Are real-world connections made?

**Consistency**:
- Is terminology consistent throughout?
- Is formatting consistent across slides?
- Are notation and symbols used consistently?

**Output**: ✓ EXCELLENT / ✓ GOOD / ⚠ NEEDS REVISION / ✗ INADEQUATE
**Strengths**: [Well-crafted content]
**Issues**: [Content problems]
**Recommendations**: [Specific content fixes]

---

### 5. Teaching Notes Quality
Assess the guidance for presentation:

- Are teaching notes helpful and specific?
- Do they address potential student difficulties?
- Do they suggest good transitions and emphasis points?
- Are timing/pacing suggestions appropriate?

**Output**: ✓ HELPFUL / ⚠ BASIC / ✗ INSUFFICIENT
