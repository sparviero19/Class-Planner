Guidelines for Slide Generation:
* Structure: Create a distinct section in the presentation for each topic.
* Section Length: Each section must contain at least 6 slides, but can include more if necessary.
* Total Length: The presentation should be between 25 and 30 slides in total, excluding section dividers/title slides.
* Content Focus: Do not include slides for bibliography, exercises, or other material better suited for the printed notes.
* Text Style: The text should be succinct but not limited to bullet points. Include longer explanations where necessary for clarity.
* Visuals: The slides must contain figures. Prioritize figures from the provided supplemental materials (slides from other courses, book chapters).
* External Images: If no provided image is suitable for a specific slide, propose a new image by describing its content or providing a link to an external resource (e.g., the web).
